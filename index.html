    The artists

    Two stereoscopic eyes on America

    Arthur Jafa

    b. 1960, Tupelo, Mississippi · lives in Los Angeles

    Filmmaker, cinematographer (Daughters of the Dust) and artist. He builds montage out of found images of Black American life, aiming for a Black cinema with the power and beauty of Black music. Golden Lion, Venice Biennale 2019.

    Richard Prince

    b. 1949, Panama Canal Zone · lives in New York

    Started rephotographing ads in 1977, working "without a licence." Cowboys, biker girlfriends, jokes, nurses and Instagram posts: he collects what American culture throws away and blows it up into art, which has led to famous court cases.
